About Me ↓

Dhyey

The Builder Behind the Portfolio

I'm Dhyey Patel, a developer and founder from India who blends building with design to craft products that don't just look good — they ship, and they work.

My Background

I started with curiosity, grew through shipping, and now build products where AI is the hard core — not a wrapper. Every project is a chance to learn, experiment, and make something people actually use.

  • B.Tech CSE — VIT Bhopal (2025–2029)
  • Editor — GSoC Innovators Club, VIT Bhopal

What Drives Me

  • Build Real Things — working products over demos and mockups.
  • Ship Over Show — a live link beats a pitch, every time.
  • Clarity First — if it isn't clear, it isn't finished.
  • Always Learning — every build teaches the next one.
  • Build With Purpose — effort should turn into proof, and into value.

How I Work

My approach is simple, but it works: I start by understanding the problem and who it's for. Then I build fast, verify on the live product itself — never on claims — and ship.
